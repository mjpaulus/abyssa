From the 2026-09-01 design evaluation (ABYSSA Soundings). Effort: S-M.

## Detail
PROBLEM: The one lethal resource is almost never the thing threatened. O2 refills at 0.28/s while supplied, so a 10% bite is erased in 0.4s — a camera shake, not a stake. Leviathan contact drains LIGHT, which regenerates. The air bar is decorative; fuel is the real bar and it has no hierarchy.

CHANGE: Bite → TORN DRESS state: O2 refill halved for ~20s so 'AIR IS LEAKING' is true and the tenders can't out-pump the hole. Leviathan contact does the same. Turns predators and the sleeper into decisions about retreating toward the line. Pairs with shark-bite-lantern-flicker.
