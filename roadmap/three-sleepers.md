Michael: the leviathan is boring, one serpent scaled three ways. Each zone gets its own colossus — crab, octopus and squid characteristics, not those animals — monolithic next to Sal, different strengths and weaknesses. Something discoverable in each level triggers the creature (the trigger is also the reward), so the world gets bigger and more discoverable. Wards stay the verb; the payoff differs per zone.

## Detail
Full design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-three-sleepers-design.md. Zone 0 VELKATH THE BROODER, buried as the ridge, woken by taking an egg from her nest; wards underneath, seen only when she stands; the last ward lights when the eggs go back. Zone 1 ORUNE THE HOARDER, lairing in the trawler's hoard of drowned lights; woken by taking the ship's lamp; takes the hose, hates light; you keep the lamp and she becomes a lit landmark. Zone 2 MHOR THE HUNTER, drawn up from below when you refuel the dead furnace; speed and reach, stunned by the flare; payoff is passage. Each zone gets a 3-4 point discoverable chain pencilled on the chart. Base/creature module split first, then one creature at a time, serpent stays until replaced. Folds in octopus-lantern-theft and resolves hose-leash-decision (clamp with give = her pull).
