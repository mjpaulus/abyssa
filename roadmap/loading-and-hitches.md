A real loading screen whose bar is driven by the actual boot work (module fetch, world build steps, each sculpted asset set, physics, audio, shader compile, warm-up frames), and the root-cause fix for the chunky seconds after the title click and after Sal's first entry into the water.

## Detail
Michael (verbatim): "when the game is loading, show a progress bar of what is happening. After I click the screen everything is super chunky, it settles after a moment and performance improves. It happens again after sal enters the water."

- Progress must be monotonic and reach 100% only when the game is truly ready to play smoothly.
- Design like the game: brass/ink/paper, never a web spinner, never neon; reduced motion respected; hands off to the title.
- Goal: after 100%, the click into play and the first dive have no frame over ~2x the steady frame time.
- Measure with the headless bench host (tools/bench/cdp.mjs) + tools/bench/hitch.mjs (real mouse click, real W key).

## Log
- 2026-10-04 — created (branch loadbar). Baseline measured (bench host, 60 Hz vsync): the click stalls ~2 s (a 614 ms task, then ~1.4 s more): programs 306 -> 422 because the ward-light pool (5 PointLights) is built by the first enterZone(0) AT THE CLICK, taking the scene from 9 to 14 lights and recompiling every lit material (getProgramInfoLog 1.3 s), plus the Brooder's build (418 ms), audio graph (200 ms), shadow-depth programs and Rapier init. The first dive stalls ~700 ms (200/350/150 ms frames) inside the sky's PBO readback: GPU-process pipeline creation for draws first seen underwater (only 2 new programs). DRS then stepped 1.5 -> 0.875 on the hitch misses.
- 2026-10-04 — SHIPPED on branch loadbar (merged with main 8961ebf). THE LOADER: a brass scale on ink (engraved rule, ticks, a brass band that only advances, an index needle, one ALL-CAPS line of what the tender is doing: TAKING ON STORES / RAISING THE SEABED / MIXING THE WATER / SEWING THE DRESS / GRINDING THE GLASS / SOUNDING THE DEPTHS / TRIMMING THE LAMPS ...), driven by real work: module fetch (resource count vs the last load's), each world-build step, each sculpted asset set as it lands (assets.js ledger), physics, plants, audio, compile per scene branch, warm-up cameras, title settle frames; 100 only after the warm-up. Monotonic (probed). Reduced motion: no transitions. ROOT CAUSES FIXED, all moved under the loader: (1) zone 0 + the 5 ward lights built at boot (enterZone quiet) -> 14 lights before any compile; (2) the boot compile ran with no render target bound, so three built the canvas sRGB+ACES variants no draw uses -- it now issues against the composer's buffer, at BOTH sun-shadow states; (3) warm-up frames through the real path (sky -> refraction -> composer) from six cameras (deck, waterline, column, deep, seabed, looking up) with everything drawn (distant casters' shadows off), building ANGLE pipelines, shadow-depth and clip-plane programs and texture uploads; (4) Rapier, props, every sculpted set and the plant batches awaited; (5) audio noise + room IRs prebaked at 48k/44.1k (bit-identical, copied at the click). MEASURED (bench host, vsync 60, same session paired vs base a64468f): click -- base 583 + 1350 ms stalls, 306 -> 422 programs; loadbar max 67 ms, programs flat (666 through click AND dive). First dive -- base 200/350/150 ms frames on a cold run; loadbar max 16.8 ms, 0 frames over 33. Loading to 100: ~7.3 s warm cache (base reached its title at ~3.6-4.9 s and then paid the stalls in play). Remaining: frames of 33-50 ms in the first seconds on deck are GPU throughput at 60 fps (scale-insensitive under another agent's GPU load: pipe 11-14 ms at 0.875-1.5x), not one-time work. Not warmed: zone 1/2 sleepers (built at their enterZone). How to see: reload the game; watch the bar; click and walk off the deck. Dev: __boot.log(), ?warmdiag, tools/bench/hitch.mjs.
