Michael: "lots of things just look like primitives no polish, even the crab beast. AAA is the bar." The audit at 30 u in daylight found the shell plates reading as a stack of smooth grey pebbles, carapace and arms one flat clay material, thorns plain cones, reef growths flat cylinders, legs tubes, eyes dots. Bar: a Monster Hunter / Shadow of the Colossus creature at close range. Geometry and materials only: behaviour, rite, timings, wards, collision (L.spine / L.collR) and the dormant/wake contract are untouched. Branch polish-brooder, not merged.

## Detail
Files: src/entities/sleeper/brooder.js, brooderGeo.js (brood.js and lib/textures.js unchanged).
- SHELL PLATES: bladeGeo(variant) is a real plate. The section is a flat top that breaks over a chamfer into a thin rim, with a flat underside. The root is thick and the tip thin. The outline is an angular shard (a broad shoulder to a long point). Each of 4 variants has its own broken-edge profile: V notches on either margin, a fine serration, and on some a snapped jagged tip. Vertex-colour AO marks the root cavity and the underside. There are 4 InstancedMeshes with a per-plate tint. bladeMaps() is a 512x512 atlas, one 512x128 strip per variant, holding growth shelves (a sawtooth of steps), growth rings, ribs, pitting, edge chips, rust rims and wet-ridge/matte-groove roughness. castShadow and receiveShadow are on.
- CARAPACE MAPS: carapaceMaps() now bakes into typed arrays and DataTextures, with no canvas round trip. Heights stay float until the normal is taken, which avoids 8-bit terracing. Analytic fields (h, seam distance, corner f3-f1, scute id and a rust-run field) are computed at 320^2 and sampled bilinearly. Detail noise comes from 128^2 tileable fbm tables. Features: a V seam with real depth; a bevel and crown on each scute; growth lines parallel to the margin; ragged chips that expose a paler under-layer, most at triple corners; lichen crust with height; stamped sponge bores with raised bleached rims; rust pooling in the seams and streaking downhill from them; moss only where the cavity mask (blur(h) - h) is high; per-scute tint; wet crowns (0.24) against matte crust (0.9). Resolution is 1024 because 2048 would not fit the bake budget.
- ARMS / LEGS / MOUTH: chitinMaps() is a 1024x512 atlas with this UV contract: u in [0, 0.5) is a limb segment, running from a pale wrinkled membrane through grey-teal stippled chitin with teal and pale flecks and scratches to a sutured, rust-stained cuff; u in [0.5, 1) is a finger, running from chitin through rust pooling in pits to dark glossy horn. The pincer rust is now a map, not vertex colour. chitinSheen() adds a lit Fresnel rim (scaled by the diffuse light received, so it is never a glow in the dark). It uses one customProgramCacheKey, shared by arms, legs (charcoal tint) and mouthparts. Tubercles are lathed bosses with a rim and a cratered cap. Spikes and teeth are fluted chitin spikes with a flared base and a bone-to-horn gradient. Segments are articulated: the membrane neck starts inside the previous cuff, the cuff swells with a lipped rim, and the ends are domed knuckles with smoothed cap UVs. Arm roots are buried under the lip, and fingers have knob rows.
- REEF: reefGeo() is ONE merged mesh with vertex colours, where there used to be 4 meshes. It holds lathed tube sponges (flared foot, lumpy pitted wall, lipped osculum, dark throat), gorgonian fans (forking branches fused into a lattice by nearest-node bridges) and encrusting sponge mats moulded to the shell with dark pores. Pigments are muted.
- FACE: a dark brow plate, merged into the carapace geometry, carries the eight eyes in carved sockets with raised rims. Each eye is a wet black dome with a thin gold ring (eyeGeo). The eye shader masks the eyeshine emissive and mirror-wetness to the dome by vertex colour. The front visor now stops above the eye cluster. Mouthparts carry a comb of fine setae (fringe).
- WEED: tapered, folded fronds instead of flat strips.
