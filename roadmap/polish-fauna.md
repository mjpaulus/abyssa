Michael's verdict: "lots of things just look like primitives no polish. AAA is the bar." The sea life below the jellies was flat-shaded: untextured wedge fish, smooth-loft ray and turtle, single-colour crabs/stars/urchins, a painted shark, a blob octopus, cone squid. This pass gives every animal a generated skin, real eyes, and anatomy where the silhouette was a primitive. Materials, geometry detail and shader surface terms only — no behaviour, FSM, steering, spawn counts, events or reseed contracts touched.

## Detail
Files: `src/world/fauna.js`, `creatures.js`, `predators.js`, `ventlife.js`. No textures are baked: every map is procedural in-shader.
- **Skin kit** (fauna.js `SKIN_COMMON` / `SKIN_LIGHTS`, imported by the other three): hashes, value noise, Worley, roof-tile fish scales, relief by screen derivatives (perturbNormalArb maths fed a procedural height, so no tangents and no texture reads), fwidth anti-aliasing, a thin-sheet **transmission** term and a wet-dome **catchlight** that read the lights already in the scene (no lights added), and a hemisphere environment for silver flanks.
- **School fish** (one program, `abyssa-fish-skin`): profiled bodies (2.3x rings, +4 sides, seam column), roof-tiled scales with countershading, saddles, operculum groove, lateral-line pores, iridescence (fresnel-weighted hue walk), a mirror flank term, wet eyes (iris ring, pupil, catchlight), fins with jointed rays, a scalloped free edge and backlit translucency. Zone-0 photophore rows cut to a faint stripe (sunlit reef fish carry none). New `aSurf` attribute; the undulation still keys off `uv`.
- **Fauna** (still one program, `abyssa-fauna-skin`): per-vertex surface kind (`aKind`: skin, cornea, iris, fin, mouth, tooth, belly, leg, spine, shell) + a per-material skin id. Ray: dorsal spots, cream belly, mottled spec, dermal grain. Turtle: 13 scutes with seam grooves, growth rings, tortoiseshell streaks, marginals; scaled head and flippers. Moray: reticulation and mottle, slick; a wet blood-red mouth with ivory teeth. Crab: granular carapace, pale underside, jointed legs with arthrodial cuffs and dark dactyls, a fixed finger on the chela. Star: ossicle net, two tube-foot rows per arm. Urchin: tubercle bosses in socket rings, banded glossy spines. Vent fish/lanternfish scales, flapjack papillae, isopod pitted chitin, angler/gulper velvet with pores. Every eye is an iris bead + a wet cornea with catchlight.
- **Shark**: 110 rings x 36 sides with the rings spent on the head and gill field; a Catmull-Rom profile; five gill slits cut as real grooves with standing flaps; an underslung crescent mouth groove with two serrated tooth rows (52 teeth); a domed wet eye; fins rebuilt as thick falcate solids. Skin: ragged countershade line, fbm mottle, denticle ridges + a Kajiya-Kay anisotropic sheen along the bent body axis, healed rake scars sunk and pale.
- **Octopus**: mantle 28x28, arms 30x12 with a flattened oral face; papillae that rise with arousal, chromatophores that open as it flushes, two staggered sucker rows (pale raised rim, dark acetabulum) shrinking to the tip, brass-iris eyes with a horizontal slit pupil. At rest the arms lie out across the silt (vertex shader only).
- **Squid**: mantle continues into a head with two big silvered eyes; rhomboid 6x4 fins lit through from behind; 7-sided arms and clubbed tentacles; pulsing chromatophores; photophores as discrete lamps in four ventral rows under the existing strobe; the existing pooled glow sprites (5 per squid, 20 across the shoal, fog-faded) moved to the ventral line and shrunk.
- **Vent life**: shrimp (~60 tris) with rostrum, eyes, antennae, tail fan, a glassy carapace (segment overlaps, gut, chromatophores) and the vent fire transmitted through the body; vent crabs (~550 tris) carry the reef crab's skin on the sulfide palette.
