From the 2026-09-01 design evaluation (ABYSSA Soundings). Effort: S.

## Detail
PROBLEM: Descending into a rift bowl with the sleeper uncalmed drops the player into total darkness at -318 with no message — an unmarked hole in the seabed.

CHANGE: One-shot showMsg when the player descends >20u into a bowl with the sleeper awake: 'THE RIFT IS SHUT WHILE IT WAKES.' Plus a faint floor glow in the bowl so the hole reads as a place, not a fall-through.
