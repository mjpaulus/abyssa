## Detail
- **Sleepers** (`sleeper/brooder.js`, `hoarder.js`, `hunter.js`): Velkath rides her planted feet and dips on each footfall. Her wake is a staged sequence, and her hammer blow has a cock, a trembling hold and a slam. Orune's arms curl as travelling waves over verlet chains; her grab wraps Sal and her eyes track him. Mhor swims in jet pulses with a glide between them, with verlet arms and ballistic tentacles; when stunned he convulses, then goes limp. `ev.quake` drives camera shake. Strike windows are unchanged.
- **Sal** (`entities/diver.js` + camera section of `game.js`): turns lead with the helmet, he looks at the nearest creature or the sleeper, and idles are a valve check or peering with the lantern raised. Hit, slam and grab recoils, burst jolt, low-air hunch, hose pull. Per-foot slope IK brings the boots to within 2.5 cm of the ground (was 10.6 cm). Turning on the spot steps. The blue emissive pack light is now a brass pressure gauge. The camera gets a footfall dip, a landing sag and look-ahead. `__sal`.
- **Fauna and flora** (`world/stir.js` new, fauna/creatures/predators/ventlife/gardens/flora): one shared disturbance source. Boids school, mill as a hollow ring, part round Sal and flash-expand when startled. Shark hunch and gape. Ray wing wave, turtle strokes, moray lunges, crabs scuttle sideways. One world current sways every plant; plants part round movers and flinch at footfalls. Sleeper hammer blows and slams stir the reef (game.js).
- **Lighting and grade** (`lib/surface.js` new, `lighting.js` relight, `postfx.js` film finish): light from point sources loses red first on its way through the water, plus wrap diffuse, a backscatter rim, a wet film, thin-sheet transmission, and the water's colours as environment. The lantern is the key light and the cyan fill is cut. The deck is warm. Film finish per zone; zone 2 is indigo-slate, not violet. AO per regime. `__look`, `__surf`, `__film`, `__aok`.
- **Atmosphere** (`world/water.js`, `world/particulate.js` new, volumetrics, skyrays): the lantern's glow in the water is a closed-form single-scattering integral, plus one more slot for the nearest vent, ward or hoard lamp. Marine snow is lit by the real lantern. Bokeh motes, plankton that sparks in the hand's wake, sea spray. God rays carry caustic filaments that sway with the swell. Each zone has its own snow character. Sky gradient and far haze. The pale ridge on the deck horizon was the refraction pass painting sky and is fixed. `syncLamps()`.
- **Perf** (`core.js`, `postfx.js`): measured at 1.5x render scale on Retina, the GPU median was about 19 ms against a 16.7 ms slot; 1.0x saves about 6 ms. DYNAMIC RESOLUTION now keeps the GPU median inside 55–78% of the governor's slot by moving the render scale between 0.85 and 1.5. It settles at 1.125x and about 12 ms here. `__drs.state()`, `__drs.pin(x)`.
- **Grain** (`lib/surface.js`, `postfx.js`, `world/terrain.js`): film grain under water cut from 0.045 to 0.026. At the old amount it was roughly half the pixel-level noise in dark areas. Kaplanyan–Hoffman specular AA on every lit material (`__specAA`). The 1.6 u sand grain fades once a texel falls under a pixel.
