## 1. What the game is

ABYSSA is a 3D browser game in Three.js: "Shadow of the Colossus at the bottom of the sea".

- **The player** is **Sal**, a hard-hat diver in a Mark V brass helmet and weighted canvas suit. His air comes down a hose from a pump on a raft at the surface. The pump burns bitumen; the hose is lengthened with polymer. Both are gathered from the seabed.
- **The goal.** He descends a three-zone trench (luminous shallows, twilight, near-black abyss). In each zone a colossal **sleeper** bars the way. He calms it, not kills it, by lighting the **wards** on its body. Calming it opens a **fissure** (the rift) to the next zone. Calming the last one plays the ending (a 75 s ascent with the three sleepers passing).
- **The three sleepers** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-three-sleepers-design.md`): each has crab, octopus or squid characteristics, without being that animal.
  - **Zone 0, home: VELKATH THE BROODER**, a crab the size of a house. All recent work is on her (section 9).
  - **Zone 1: ORUNE THE HOARDER** (octopus-like). She lairs in a trawler's hoard of drowned lights, takes the hose and hates light. Shipped.
  - **Zone 2: MHOR THE HUNTER** (squid-like). He is drawn up when the dead furnace is refuelled, and is fast with long reach. Shipped.
  - The old serpent leviathan survives only as a fingerprint (`__lev.fp`) and in chart data.
- **The world around them:**
  - Fish schools, jellies, a shark that circles fast or bright movers, an octopus that steals his lantern light, and squid in the abyss.
  - Three wrecks hold three tools: the sonar, a spear gun, and an air thruster.
  - A deterministic 12-minute day with storms. The raft is a real place he stands on.
  - **The air pack:** Space is a jet fed by a reserve tank the pump refills. A tap hops, holding it bursts.
- **The harpoon.** Velkath has an old harpoon in her shell. Pulling it wakes her, and from then on it is Sal's two-handed thrust weapon (left click). This replaced the egg-taking trigger in October.
- **Anchorages.** An anchorage is a place the raft sails to from the chart table. There is **home** plus three others: Pallid Bank, the Burned Ground, and the hidden Unsounded Shelf. Each has its own water, light, seabed and wrecks, and harder versions of the same three sleepers. **Michael parked the other three on 2026-10-15**: "would want to ignore the other anchorages for now... we should focus on getting one right now." They must keep working (their probes and fingerprints still run) but get no tuning or polish. He sees them as future "missions", each unique. Not designed yet: card `roadmap/anchorages-as-missions.md`.
- **Saving.** Progress saves to localStorage (`abyssa.chart.v1`). The title screen has START OVER (since 2026-10-26).

## 2. Tech and how to run it

- **Stack:** Three.js **r184**, postprocessing 6.39, n8ao 1.9, all through a CDN importmap in `index.html`. No build step, no bundler: ES modules and a static server. The README still says r160; that is stale.
- **Repo:** `/Users/michaelpaulus/sc/abyssa`, GitHub `mjpaulus/abyssa` (branch `main`). License GPL-3.0. About 88k lines of JS in `src/`. 1,264 commits from 2026-07-30 to now.
- **Serve:** `python3 serve.py <port>`. It sends no-store headers; plain `http.server` lets the browser serve stale modules.
- **Play:** `http://localhost:<port>/`. Add `?playtest` for the playtest keys. For example, **Alt+6** jumps to Velkath's harpoon and **Alt+=** grants or revokes the harpoon.
  - Full list: card `roadmap/playtest-jumps.md` and `src/ui/playtest.js`.
  - `?bench` is for the bench harness.
- **Offline gate:** `node --check` on a `.mjs` copy of each edited file. **The browser is the only trusted loader.** A backtick inside a comment inside a GLSL template string ends the string: node accepts it, the browser throws. Never put backticks in GLSL comments.
- **Controls:**

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Look |
| WASD | Walk or swim |
| Space | Air pack (tap = hop, hold = burst) |
| C | Sink |
| Shift | Faster walk |
| Left click | Knife slash, or the harpoon thrust once he has it |
| Right click | Spear gun |
| Q | Ink |
| T | Sonar |
| E | Take, use, pull |
| P | Bypasses all post-processing (the standard A/B for any rendering artifact) |

**Enforced conventions** (CLAUDE.md, same section):
- No per-frame allocation in hot paths.
