### How clawFrame works

1. **Window.** It runs only while `PH_COCK0 <= L.hamPh < PH_SLAM0`, that is the cock (0.40-0.72) and the hold (to 0.84).
2. **Gates.** All of these must hold, or the target is 0:
   - she is a brooder, awake, not calmed, and `standE > 0.5`;
   - not paused;
   - `deckK == 0`, and not `throatOn`;
   - `bossCool == 0`, so no look in the last 0.6 s;
   - the blow is not latched off by a look.
3. **Weights.** The target is scaled by:
   - `smoothstep(0.5, 0.9, threatE)`;
   - `1 - smoothstep(0.1, 0.5, creUnder)`;
   - `1 - smoothstep(36, 42, d)`, where d is his distance from her centre;
   - BOSSCAM's front test, applied to her body (her belly point, as `bossF`). It uses the body, not the claw, because early in the cock the claw is over or behind the lens while she is ahead.
4. **Target.** Take the angle of her major claw's palm above the lens's forward. The palm is the middle of the propodus, wrist (`c.pj`) to finger hinge (`c.dj`), read from `matrixWorld`; her pose step leaves those current, and they are only read inside the window. From that, subtract what puts the palm at NDC y 0.7. Cap the result at `maxDeg`.
5. **Sal stays in frame.** The tilt that would put his bonnet's lowest point below NDC y -0.92 is the hard limit. It applies to the target and to the spring's output.
   - The point is the live helmet: `diver.helmGroup`'s origin (its neck ring, about 0.06 u over the lowest vertex), as the rig posed it last frame, less `helmLo` 0.12.
   - The first version used a fixed `player.pos.y + 0.8`. On the 30 u spot's slope his body sits lower, and the real rim slipped to NDC -0.956 on 12 of 319 frames. The final probe caught it, and c385d18 fixed it.
6. **Spring.** A critically damped spring, using the exact step for a target held over the frame, so it is stable at any dt and the rate is the real rate. It runs at `wIn` 7 in the window and `wOut` 12 outside it, so the return starts on the fall's first frame. It snaps to exactly 0.
7. **Applied** as `camera.rotateOnWorldAxis(horizontal right, tilt)`: pitch only, no yaw, roll kept. The boom, the lens position, `camAim` and `camLook` are untouched.

## Deviations from the brief, and why

1. **Applied as the lens's last rotation, not by rotating `camAim`.**
   - This is still "after the lean, as a pitch about the lens", just at the end of `updateCamera`, after the lean, the roll and the yank's nod.
   - Rotating `camAim` would leave a residue in `camLook`: its 40/s lerp carries it for 2-3 frames after a mouse drop.
   - As the last rotation instead:
     - the drop is complete on the input frame;
     - the off path is exactly the main lens (it never writes shared state);
     - `__clawcam.state().qPre` is exactly the on:0 lens on every frame, so every "vs on:0" number below is a same-frame comparison with no run-to-run noise.
   - The lens position does not depend on the aim (no system downstream of `camAim` moves it), so this is exact.
2. **The cap is 25°, not 12-15°.**
   - Measured on main's own frames (the what-if below), 12-15° frames *nothing* of her claw at any front spot.
   - At the hold, the claw is 51-78° above the lens.
   - 25° puts her wrist or finger on screen through the hold at her standoff, and the palm on about a third of the cock and hold. It is a knob.
3. **The range is "full to 36 u, gone by 42 u", not a cut at BOSSCAM.r (30 u).**
   - Un-held, with Sal still 45 u ahead and no input, she closed to **33-35 u** and hammered from there: 10 landings in 40 s. Her `closeK` stops her at HUNT.hold 2.05 R (31.6 u) off where she thinks he is.
   - A 30 u cut switched the feature off exactly where she swings.
   - BOSSCAM's own ramp ends at 36 u. Knobs: `rNear`, `rFar`.
4. **The spots.**
   - At 10 u from her centre he is under her shell. Her shell half-width R is 15.4 u, and `STAND.under` is 0.85 R = 13.1 u. Rule 5 turns the look-up off there, and in play she does not hammer there (`L.under` zeroes her threat). So 10 u is a gate test: the look-up must be exactly 0.
   - 14 and 18 u are booked, not asserted, because the claw is unreachable there (see below).
   - I added 24, 30 and 34 u (34 is her standoff) and one spot off her side: 18 u ahead, 12 u to her left.
5. **The video is not from riteplay.**
   - The rite policy writes `player.yaw` and `player.pitch` every slice, which is a look input by BOSSCAM's rule. In the perfect runs the look-up never engaged (`ccN` 0), so a riteplay before/after shows nothing.
   - The before/after is the probe's still stand at her standoff, the same three blows.
6. **The mouse test writes the look input directly.** Headless Chrome has no pointer lock, so `player.yaw -= 4 * 0.0022` stands in for a 4 px mousemove. That is exactly what `player.js`'s handler writes.

How to read it:
- **Run-to-run noise is large.** Main alone ran 20.5 s and 107.8 s on the same average seed. The sim draws `Math.random`; the seed drives only the policy.
- **Every branch number falls inside main's own spread or the ritefair range** (average 45-165 s, 0-3 hits). Hammer hits were 0 in every run, main and branch. The hits came from drops, sweeps and a leg.
- **The perfect policy never sees the look-up.** It writes `player.yaw` and `player.pitch` every 0.25 s, which is a look input, so `bossCool` and the latch keep it off: `ccOff` 995-1134 window frames per run. Its fight runs with the main camera exactly.

It targets only wards it has *seen* on screen (`PROBE.seen`: the camera frustum), so the look-up can change which ward it goes for. Average-mode differences are therefore not purely noise.
