## Findings and concerns

1. **Some fullplay fights run differently under machine load (a "pressed" mode), on baa7f88 too.**

   **What it looks like.** In each batch started with 4-6 probe Chromes at once, 1-2 runs play a different fight:
   - the second ward is lit about 1.5 s after the first;
   - Sal fights pressed under her (pressed% 38-49);
   - the lens is pushed into Sal and into her shell (baa7f88's lmBase3: 42 fight-time inside frames, 15 in Sal);
   - sometimes one hit lands.

   **Evidence it is load, not code.**
   - It happened on **pristine baa7f88** in the matched-load test (lmBase3-1).
   - It happened with velfix's pre-calm path, which is baa7f88's (gpcA-1 / gpcA-2).
   - Load even moves the approach: the take is at 30.8-31.3 s under load and 32.0-32.8 s with one or two runs going.
   - The quality ladder is ruled out: `__perf.state().stage` is 0 in every run, now recorded in the ledger. The
     mechanism is still unidentified, possibly a wall-clock dependence before the stepped loop takes over.

   **What velfix leaves in that mode.**
   - Post-calm: the lens is 0 frames inside her (gpcA-1/2).
   - **The wash is not fully gone**: 6-7 booked frames over 5% blown outside the burn discs (max 0.16-0.20), where the
     lens is pressed against Sal at a lit ward.
   - The same mode on baa7f88: 206 such frames, max 0.23 outside and 0.80 in all.
   - So those two runs are RED on the wash. They are the only RED velfix runs once she stopped lying down on him.

   **A correction.** I first blamed camera changes for the mode (the messages of 3278219 / f229568 / 8bfaa29). That
   was confounded by load. The swing, a minD->minIn last resort and a fight-time look-ahead were judged partly in
